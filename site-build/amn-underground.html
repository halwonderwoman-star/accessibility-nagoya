ESCA

Topics3

Elevators

The only lift up to street level is beside stair E9, on the station side — it runs between the B1 mall and the 1F street entrance toward JR Nagoya Station’s Taiko-dori/Shinkansen side. Two more lifts, near E2 and E6, only reach B2 parking, not the street.

Sourced from ESCA’s official floor guide. E1–E17 are ESCA’s own stair labels; not every stair is confirmed to reach street level.

Toilets

The southwest toilets, near E1–E2, have a multipurpose accessible toilet alongside the men’s and women’s rooms. The northwest toilets, near E7, have a baby-changing bed but are not marked accessible on the official map.

Coin lockers

Lockers are reported in the connecting passage on the left, just after coming down the stairs or escalator from the JR Taiko-dori exit into ESCA — near stair E9, close to the street-level lift.

From a third-party locker-finder site, not ESCA’s own floor guide — treat the exact spot as approximate.

Getting thereKasadera, 5 min walk

JR Tokaido Main Line, Kasadera Station, is the only station named on the official Aichi Prefecture access page — no bus route is listed for this venue. There is no general spectator or bicycle parking, but a set of reservable accessible spaces is provided.

AJR Tokaido Main Line · Kasadera Station
About 5 min walk. Walking directions (Google Maps)

ElevatorThree lifts, no escalators

Three separate elevators serve this station’s four platforms, one per platform group — not one lift for the whole station. JR Central’s own barrier-free table confirms all three; there is no escalator anywhere in the station.

APlatform 1
Tokaido Main Line, up direction. Elevator confirmed; no escalator.
BPlatforms 2·3
Tokaido Main Line, both directions. Elevator confirmed; no escalator.
CPlatform 4
Tokaido Main Line, down direction. Elevator confirmed; no escalator.
Ask before you travel
JR Central notes that some passages between gate and platform may not be usable at certain times of day or for certain trains — contact the station in advance to ask which gate suits your platform best.

Accessible toiletOne toilet, inside the gates

Only one wheelchair-accessible toilet exists at this station, and it is inside the gates — there is none outside. JR Central’s own toilet table confirms wheelchair access, a baby-seat fitting and an ostomate fitting.

AInside the gates
Wheelchair-accessible, with a baby-seat fitting and an ostomate fitting — confirmed.
Outside the gates
No accessible toilet outside the gates at this station.

Entrances & exitsOne gate area

JR Central does not name multiple gates for this station (no separate North/South exit listed) — treat it as a single ticket-gate area. Ticket window hours are 5:30–22:00, though some hours may see partial closures.

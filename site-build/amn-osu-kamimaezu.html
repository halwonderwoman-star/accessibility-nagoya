Elevators & step-free access

North, South and West gates sit on B1. EV.1 and EV.2 reach Meijo’s B2 platforms from South Gate; EV.3 and EV.4 reach Tsurumai’s B3 platforms from West Gate. Transferring between the two lines stays step-free via the B2 passage — for Meijo platform 1, whose own approach has stairs, use EV.1 to the paid South Gate area, then EV.2. A public street elevator, EV.5, reaches the northwest corner of the Kamimaezu-higashi intersection from a ramp between West and South gates.

Sourced from Nagoya City Transportation Bureau’s official Meijo and Tsurumai diagrams and accessibility data for Kamimaezu, checked 14 September 2026.

Toilets

Two accessible toilets are outside South Gate, alongside general restrooms; more general restrooms are outside West Gate and in the paid B2 transfer passage. The official guide notes steps at each toilet approach.

Sourced from Nagoya City Transportation Bureau’s toilet guide for Kamimaezu, checked 14 September 2026.

Step-free guide

Bays1–11

ElevatorエレベーターTwo, at the edges

There is no lift inside the boarding loop itself — the two nearest lifts are at the concourse’s two connections: JR Gate Tower to the south, and KITTE to the east.

AJR Gate Tower, south side
Down to B1F, next to the arrivals / drop-off concourse.
BKITTE 1F
East side. Enter here for bays 1–6.
Also nearby
A further lift continues down to the Higashiyama Line via B1 / GateWalk — a subway connection, not part of the bus terminal itself.

One accessible toilet at each end of the concourse.

Entrances & exits出入口Sakuradori side

Not on this map
Curbside stops 21/22 and the Taikodori-side stops 33/34 sit outside this main loop concourse.
Coin lockers
Yes — inside the terminal, south side: 89 lockers (small ×32, medium ×15, large ×26/8, extra-large ×8), cash or IC card. Two more banks are just outside, on the Sakuradori side near Dotour and inside Takashimaya 1F.

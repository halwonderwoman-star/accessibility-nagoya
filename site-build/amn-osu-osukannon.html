Elevators & step-free access

EV.1 and EV.2 connect each platform to the B1 concourse; from there, exit the ticket gate and take EV.3 up to the street, just north of Exit 2. Exit 2’s own escalator (ES.3) covers only the middle part of that stair approach, so it isn’t a step-free alternative.

Sourced from Nagoya City Transportation Bureau’s official diagram and accessibility data for Osu Kannon, checked 14 September 2026.

Toilets

Two accessible toilets and general restrooms are outside the gates, in the B1 public hall’s south end — reached by a ramp beside a three-step stair approach.

Sourced from Nagoya City Transportation Bureau’s toilet guide for Osu Kannon, checked 14 September 2026.

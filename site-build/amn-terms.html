About this site

Accessibility Map NAGOYA is an independent, non-official guide to step-free access around Nagoya — elevators, ramps, toilets and gate positions at stations, shopping streets and public facilities. It is not affiliated with any railway operator, shopping district, venue or government body named on it.

Accuracy

We check each map and page against official sources (transit operators, municipal data, venue websites) at the date shown as “Last updated” on that page. Facilities change — an elevator can go out of service, a toilet can move, a gate can close early. We make no guarantee that any page is complete, current or error-free, and using this site does not replace checking with the operator or venue directly before you travel, especially for a time-sensitive trip.

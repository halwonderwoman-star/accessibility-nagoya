Getting around 02

Getting around

Topics3

Up to street level

Both gates sit on B1. The step-free way up is the elevator near Exit 3, running 5:30am–12:30am — it lifts you straight to Kusugai-dori Park above. Exits 2 and 3 themselves also open directly onto the park, but only the elevator skips the stairs. North Gate leads to Exits 1, 5 and 6; South Gate leads to Exits 2, 3, 4 and the elevator.

Into Matsuzakaya

Exit 5’s staircase lands directly in Matsuzakaya’s B1 — stairs only. For a step-free way in, use Matsuzakaya’s own South Building lift instead; it can need an intercom call for assistance.

Into PARCO

PARCO is reached through an underground passage from the North Gate area, without going back up to street level first.

Sourced from Nagoya City Transportation Bureau’s gate-to-exit accessibility guide. We haven’t independently timed or photographed every step of these routes yet.

Toilets 03

Toilets

Types3

Station

Yabacho Station’s accessible toilets are outside the South Gate, with ramped approaches on both sides — marked in pink on the floor plan below.

Public toilets outside

Yaba Park Toilet (矢場公園トイレ), in Sakae 3-chome, is about 340m from the station.

Wheelchair access for this specific toilet is not confirmed in the city’s public-toilet open data. The station toilet above is the safer bet if step-free access matters.

Other: nearby large facilities

Yabacho sits beneath and beside Matsuzakaya Nagoya, PARCO, Marui and OPA — all open customer toilets during business hours, and Matsuzakaya’s South Building lift (mentioned above) connects straight into the store.

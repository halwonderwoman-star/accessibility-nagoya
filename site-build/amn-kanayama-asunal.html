Getting in

Asunal Kanayama connects directly to the station’s north exit — it’s immediately outside. A ramp meets the station approach on 1F, and the elevators reach 2F and the car park too.

Sourced from ASUNAL’s official floor guide and access page, checked 14 September 2026.

Toilets

Every floor has an accessible toilet. On 2F, the general restroom area next to the accessible one is women-only — the separate multipurpose cubicle there isn’t restricted.

Sourced from ASUNAL’s official facilities page, checked 14 September 2026.

Other facilities

Nagoya Bank and Seven Bank ATMs (one each), an AED at the 1F disaster-prevention center, and parking for around 100 mopeds and 1,700 bicycles.

Sourced from ASUNAL’s official facilities page, checked 14 September 2026.

Toilets

Accessible toilets are inside West Gate, inside the Central/East gates, and outside the gates at South Exit. The Central/East toilet has two accessible rooms.

Sourced from Meitetsu’s official diagram and a TOTO project page describing the Central/East toilet renovation, checked 14 September 2026.

Platform doors & gap

No platform-edge doors are listed for this line, and no platform is flagged with a reduced step or gap.

Sourced from Rakuraku Odekake Net (ECOMO Foundation), fed by Meitetsu’s own data, checked 14 September 2026.

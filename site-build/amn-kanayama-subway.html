Toilets

An accessible toilet and general restrooms are outside the gates, near Exit 3.

Sourced from Nagoya City Transportation Bureau’s own station data, checked 14 September 2026.

Platform doors & gap

All four Meijo Line platforms (1–4) have platform-edge doors, as do Meiko Line platforms 2 and 3. A step or gap is present at those same platforms, with some accommodation in place — we haven’t confirmed what kind (for example, a ramp board).

Sourced from Rakuraku Odekake Net (ECOMO Foundation), fed by Nagoya City Transportation Bureau’s own data, checked 14 September 2026.

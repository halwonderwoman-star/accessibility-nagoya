Topics2

Toilets

Accessible toilets, with ostomate fittings and a baby-changing table, are inside the ticket gates only — there isn’t one outside.

Sourced from Rakuraku Odekake Net (ECOMO Foundation), fed by JR Central’s own data, checked 14 September 2026.

Platform doors & gap

Only platform 3 has platform-edge doors; platforms 1, 2 and 4 don’t yet. No platform is flagged with a reduced step or gap.

Sourced from Rakuraku Odekake Net (ECOMO Foundation), fed by JR Central’s own data, checked 14 September 2026.
